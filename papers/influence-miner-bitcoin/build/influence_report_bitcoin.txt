9. Limitations of the current heuristic method
----------------------------------------------
- Labels come from cue-phrase rules (InfluenceTypeDetector, InfluenceDirectionDetector, ...); no
  gold standard has been applied yet, so precision/recall are unknown. Use
  influence_annotation_sample.csv to build one.
- A stored row is a candidate influence SIGNAL. Alignment between direction and outcome is
  descriptive association; it does not show that the sentence changed the decision.
- Roles are taken from the dataset's authorsrole2020 labels and pepdetails; most community
  members remain 'community member' or 'unknown'. Roles are only partly time-sensitive.
- Decision dates come from accrejpeps / the PEP state history; proposals without a terminal
  state have no temporal features (days_before_decision is NULL).
- Quoted text, signatures and attached PEP text are removed heuristically; some quoted
  material survives and some genuine prose inside code-like lines is dropped.
- The governance-era split is a single date; messages are not linked to the governance
  process that actually decided each PEP.
- Multi-label counts sum to more than the number of candidates.
